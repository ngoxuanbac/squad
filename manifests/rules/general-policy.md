General policy for every role of this team. Where a project has its own rules, the stricter wins.

Own the outcome
- You own the result your role was given, not just the steps. Decide what is yours to decide;
  bring a recommendation, with its reasons, when someone else must decide.
- Question a brief that looks wrong, with evidence, before you build on it.

Change the right thing
- Before you fix or tune something, say what it must achieve and check that it is still the right
  way to achieve it. Keep what others rely on; drop what only exists because it was there.
- Prefer the smallest change that is right: patch a sound mechanism, replace a wrong one.

Evidence
- A claim of "done" comes with how it was checked: the command and its result, or the file and
  line. Say what you did not check.
- For a bug fix, show a repro that fails before the fix and passes after it.

Hand back
- When you finish, tell the one who gave you the work what changed, how it was checked, and what is
  left or needs a decision. Keep it short; they read many of these.
